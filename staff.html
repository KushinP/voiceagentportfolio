How a request gets to someoneThe real path, not a diagram.

The concierge files a request with a category. That category is folded onto one of five departments — Housekeeping, Front Desk, Food & Beverage, Concierge, Maintenance — and anything unrecognised goes to Front Desk, so a request is never left unrouted.

Whoever is in today and first on the department list is assigned the request on the record, then texted if they have a number and emailed if they have an address. Every attempt is written down, including the ones that fail.

Marking someone Absent is not cosmetic: the next request in that department goes to the next person instead. It clears itself overnight.
